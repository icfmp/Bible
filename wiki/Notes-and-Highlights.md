# Notes and highlights

Bible lets you keep personal study notes connected to passages.

## Write a note

While reading a passage, press `Ctrl+N` to create a passage note.

## Find your notes

Open **My notes** with `Ctrl+Shift+N`. Notes can be organized with labels and searched.

## Highlights

Passage highlighting is under active development. The exact controls and available behavior may differ between alpha builds; consult the current application interface before relying on it.

Your notes are personal data. Before reinstalling or changing installation methods, preserve your existing application data and follow the [migration guide](https://github.com/icfmp/Bible/blob/main/docs/CANAL_ALPHA_FLATPAK.md).

See [Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting).
