# Notes and highlights

Bible lets you record thoughts, questions and observations **alongside Scripture**, then find them again whenever you return to a passage. Notes and highlights are stored locally on your computer.

## Create a passage note

1. Navigate to the passage you want to study.
2. Press **`Ctrl+N`** to create a passage note, or select a verse or passage in the reading area and choose **Add a passage note** from the passage-actions toolbar.
3. Write your note in the editor.
4. Optionally add labels to organize related notes.
5. Choose **Save**.

![Passage note editor](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/icfmp/Bible/main/wiki/images/notes-editor.png)

*Write a passage note and use the formatting controls.*

Your note is associated with a biblical reference, including its original translation and versification context. You can return to it later without remembering exactly when you wrote it.

## Format your notes

The note editor supports basic **Markdown** formatting, including:

- **Bold** and *italic* text.
- Lists.
- Links.

Use the formatting buttons above the editor, or type Markdown directly. Switch to **Preview** to check how your note will look before saving.

If you close the editor with unsaved changes, Bible asks whether to save, discard or keep editing.

## Find and organize your notes

Open **My notes** from the main window or press **`Ctrl+Shift+N`**.

You can:

- Search your notes and biblical references.
- Filter using labels such as `Prayer`, `Sermon preparation` or `Questions`.
- Sort by **Most recent**, **Oldest first** or **Bible order**.
- Open an existing note to review or edit it.

![My notes list with search and organization tools](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/icfmp/Bible/main/wiki/images/notes-list.png)

*Search and organize your saved notes.*

Labels help connect notes from different passages without changing where each note belongs in Scripture.

You can also select **Notes** in the study panel (`F9`) to work with notes relevant to your current passage. See [Study panel and commentaries](Study-Panel).

## Highlight verses

Highlights are available in the current alpha.

1. Select a verse or passage in the Bible reading area.
2. Choose **Highlight** from the passage-actions toolbar.
3. Select **Yellow**, **Green** or **Blue**.

Use **Remove highlight** to clear a highlight. You can also manage highlights associated with the current passage from the study tools.

![Highlighted Bible passage and highlighting controls](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/icfmp/Bible/main/wiki/images/highlights.png)

*Mark verses with colors to make them easier to find while reading.*

Highlights apply to biblical verses and are kept separately from your notes. Their appearance and controls may continue to be refined during alpha development.

## Protect your notes

**The alpha version does not yet provide a user-facing notes backup and restore feature.** Notes are stored locally, so take care to preserve your application data before reinstalling Linux or changing installation methods. Consult the [alpha migration guide](https://github.com/icfmp/Bible/blob/main/docs/CANAL_ALPHA_FLATPAK.md) before migrating.

## Delete a note

Open an existing note, choose **Note actions → Delete note…**, and confirm the deletion. The confirmation is designed to prevent accidental removal.
