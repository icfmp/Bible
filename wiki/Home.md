# Bible — Documentation

**Read and study Scripture, simply.**

Bible is a free and open-source GNOME desktop application for reading and studying the Bible. It is designed to keep Scripture at the center, with study tools available when you need them.

> **Alpha software:** Bible is still under active development. Some features may change, and bugs are expected.

## Our approach

**Simplicity over features.** Every addition should solve a real need and fit naturally into the reading experience. We prioritize clarity, readability and a coherent interface over a long list of features. See [Project philosophy](Project-Philosophy).
