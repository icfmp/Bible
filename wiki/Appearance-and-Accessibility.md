# Appearance and accessibility

Reading should remain comfortable over long sessions.

Bible provides appearance controls for reading typography and colors, alongside fullscreen reading (`F11`). The interface follows the system language and supports English and French.

For comfortable reading:

- Choose a text size and font that suit you.
- Use a color appearance with sufficient contrast.
- Hide the study panel with `F9` when you want fewer distractions.
- Use keyboard shortcuts for frequently repeated actions.

Accessibility and visual consistency are ongoing priorities during alpha. Please [report issues](https://github.com/icfmp/Bible/issues) if a control is hard to discover, read or operate.
