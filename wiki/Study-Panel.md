# Study panel and commentaries

Bible keeps study tools close to Scripture without making them part of the reading area. Open the **Study** panel when you need them, and close it when you want to focus on the biblical text.

## Open the study panel

Press **`F9`**, or select the **Study** button in the main window, to show or hide the panel on the right.

The panel contains three sections:

| Section | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| **Notes** | Work with personal passage notes while reading. |
| **References** | Explore references related to the selected biblical passage. |
| **Commentaries** | Read an installed commentary alongside the Bible. |

Select the section you need. Bible remembers the last selected study tool and the panel width.

## Read a commentary alongside Scripture

1. Open a Bible passage in the main reading area.
2. Show the study panel with **`F9`**.
3. Select **Commentaries**.
4. Use the **Choose a commentary** selector to pick an installed resource.

The commentary appears next to the biblical text. As you navigate or scroll through the Bible, Bible can follow the passage in the commentary where a matching entry is available.

The synchronization can also work **from the commentary back to Scripture**: when you deliberately scroll through commentary entries, the Bible can move to the corresponding passage. Bible avoids treating automatic layout changes as user navigation.

Different commentaries organize their material differently. An entry may cover a single verse, several verses, a whole section, or an introduction.

## Download additional commentaries

If no commentary is available, choose **Download a commentary** in the panel, or open **Library** with **`Ctrl+L`** and browse the commentary resources.

Once downloaded, a commentary can be selected in the study panel and read offline.

See [Downloading Bibles and commentaries](Downloading-Resources) for resource browsing and licensing details.

## Navigate within a commentary

The **Commentary actions** menu provides additional controls:

- **Previous available entry** — move to the preceding commentary entry.
- **Next available entry** — move to the following entry.
- **Source and conditions** — review the commentary's identifying information and terms.
- **Download a commentary** — open the Library for more resources.

You can also use the commentary's **Read in immersive mode** action for a larger, more focused reading view.

## Notes and references

Select **Notes** to work with notes associated with your reading. The **References** section provides passage-related reference tools.

These sections are separate from commentaries: you do not need to install a commentary to open them. Some information or actions will depend on the current passage and on the resources available to Bible.

For managing personal notes in greater depth, see [Notes and highlights](Notes-and-Highlights).

## When a commentary does not match the passage

You may occasionally see messages such as:

- **No commentary for this passage** — the selected resource has no usable entry at that location.
- **Different versifications** or **No verified alignment** — the Bible and commentary use references that cannot currently be matched reliably.
- **This commentary is no longer installed** — the resource was selected previously but is no longer available locally.

These messages are intentional. Bible prefers to show that an entry is unavailable rather than display commentary for an uncertain or incorrect passage.

Try another passage or another installed commentary when this happens.
