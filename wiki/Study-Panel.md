# Study panel and commentaries

Bible keeps optional study tools separate from the reading area so you can focus on the passage.

## Open the study panel

Press `F9` to show or hide it.

Depending on the installed resources and the current alpha build, the study area can provide passage-related material such as commentaries and references.

## Add commentaries

Open **Library** (`Ctrl+L`) and download a commentary. Clarke and MHCC are useful examples for testing.

Commentary coverage varies by resource. Some passages may have no entry; differences between versification systems can also affect reference mapping. Missing content should not be mistaken for an application failure.

See [Downloading resources](Downloading-Resources).
