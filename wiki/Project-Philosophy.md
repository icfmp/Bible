# Project philosophy

## Simplicity over features

Bible aims to make reading and studying Scripture feel natural, focused and effortless.

We do not add features simply because they are possible. A feature belongs in Bible when it:

1. Solves a real reading or study need.
2. Fits coherently into the existing experience.
3. Keeps ordinary tasks easy to discover and perform.
4. Does not crowd out the biblical text.

If a feature compromises clarity or usability, we would rather leave it out or redesign it.

## Scripture first

Reading is the core experience. Notes, references and commentaries should help when needed, then get out of the way.

## Native, accessible and offline

Bible is designed for GNOME using GTK 4 and libadwaita. The interface is available in English and French. Downloaded resources remain available offline.

## Free and open source

Bible is GPL-licensed and developed in public. Support through [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/BibleStudyApp) is optional; it does not change the project's commitment to accessible software.
