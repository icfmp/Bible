# First steps

Once Bible is installed:

1. Open **Library** (`Ctrl+L`).
2. Choose a Bible translation, review its license and download it. The KJV is a useful first test.
3. Click the passage title in the header bar to select a book and chapter.
4. Read, or open another tab with `Ctrl+T`.
5. Press `F9` to reveal the study panel, where available resources can help you explore the passage.
6. Create a passage note with `Ctrl+N`, and find your notes using `Ctrl+Shift+N`.

Bible works offline with previously downloaded resources. No Bible text is bundled with the app.
