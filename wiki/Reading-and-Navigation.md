# Reading and navigation

Bible is designed to put the text first.

## Choose a passage

Click the passage title in the header bar to select a book and chapter.

## Work with tabs

Open a new tab using `Ctrl+T` to keep more than one passage available while you study.

## Focus on reading

Use `F11` for fullscreen reading. Adjust typography and colors in the appearance settings to make longer reading sessions comfortable.

## Study while reading

Press `F9` to show or hide the study panel. Downloaded commentaries can follow the selected passage where coverage and reference mapping allow.

Bible supports multiple translations and parallel reading, but the available experience may evolve during alpha.

See [Appearance and accessibility](Appearance-and-Accessibility) and [Study panel](Study-Panel).
