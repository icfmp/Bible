# Reading and navigation

Bible is designed to put **Scripture first**. You can read a single translation without distractions, keep several passages open, or bring in study tools only when you need them.

## Choose a passage

Click the **passage title in the header bar** to open the passage selector and choose a book and chapter.

You can also use:

- `Ctrl+G` — open the passage selector.
- `Ctrl+K` — go directly to a Bible reference.

The selected passage is shown in the reading area.

![Passage selector showing books, chapters and a reference field](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/icfmp/Bible/main/wiki/images/reading-selector.png)

*Choose a Bible book and chapter, or enter a reference directly.*

## Move through the text

Move naturally from one chapter to another while reading.

![Bible's standard reading view with chapter navigation and highlighted passages](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/icfmp/Bible/main/wiki/images/reading-normal.png)

*Read Scripture with clear typography, highlighted passages and chapter navigation.* Bible also provides keyboard commands for precise navigation:

| Shortcut | Action |
| --- | --- |
| `Alt+Page Up` | Previous chapter |
| `Alt+Page Down` | Next chapter |
| `Alt+Up` | Previous verse |
| `Alt+Down` | Next verse |

If you navigate away and want to revisit an earlier location, use **Back** and **Forward** in the reading history:

- `Alt+Left` — previous location.
- `Alt+Right` — next location.

## Keep several passages open

Tabs let you keep different passages available while you study. For example, you can read a Gospel in one tab and an Old Testament reference in another without losing your place.

- `Ctrl+T` — open a new tab.
- `Ctrl+W` — close the current tab.

The application saves its reading session so it can restore your tabs and selected passages when you return, subject to the usual limitations of alpha software.

## Read another translation

Bible can use different downloaded translations. Open **Library** (`Ctrl+L`) to obtain additional versions.

A **Compare translations** control is available in the reader. It helps you view an additional translation alongside the current passage. Its presentation and behavior may continue to evolve during alpha.

![Parallel Bible reading with two translations side by side](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/icfmp/Bible/main/wiki/images/reading-parallel.png)

*Compare two translations of the same passage side by side.*

For more on available texts, see [Downloading Bibles and commentaries](Downloading-Resources).

## Focus on reading

Use **Immersive mode** (`F11`) when you want to concentrate on the text. This is Bible's dedicated distraction-reduced reading experience, rather than merely a generic window-fullscreen command.

![Immersive Bible reading with a spacious two-column layout](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/icfmp/Bible/main/wiki/images/reading-immersive.png)

*Immersive reading keeps the biblical text at the center of the screen.*

You can also open the **Reading appearance** control (`Aa`) to adjust the presentation of the text. See [Appearance and accessibility](Appearance-and-Accessibility) for details.

## Study while you read

Press `F9` or use the **Study** control to show or hide the panel on the right. With suitable downloaded resources, you can consult commentaries related to the passage you are reading.

Commentary coverage differs from one resource to another, and reference mapping is not always identical. A missing commentary entry does not necessarily mean something is wrong.

See [Study panel and commentaries](Study-Panel) for more information.

## Quick reference

| Task | Shortcut |
| --- | --- |
| Choose a passage | `Ctrl+G` |
| Go to a Bible reference | `Ctrl+K` |
| New tab | `Ctrl+T` |
| Close tab | `Ctrl+W` |
| Previous / next chapter | `Alt+Page Up` / `Alt+Page Down` |
| Previous / next verse | `Alt+Up` / `Alt+Down` |
| Reading history back / forward | `Alt+Left` / `Alt+Right` |
| Show or hide study panel | `F9` |
| Immersive mode | `F11` |
